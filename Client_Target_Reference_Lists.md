# TargetOrate Evaluation Criteria \& Parameters

## 1\. Technology Stack Parameters (Criterion 3)

### Required Technologies (Must be present for Band 10/8)

* **CRM / Sales Engagement:** Salesforce CRM, HubSpot Enterprise, or Apollo.io
* **Cloud Infrastructure:** AWS, Microsoft Azure, or Google Cloud Platform (GCP)
* **Data / Analytics:** Snowflake, Databricks, or Segment

### Preferred Technologies

* **Intent / ABM Data Providers:** 6sense, Demandbase, ZoomInfo Enterprise
* **Marketing Automation:** Marketo, Pardot (Marketing Cloud Account Engagement)

### Incompatible / Competing Stacks (Band 2)

Proprietary legacy on-premise databases without cloud connectors, or direct competing ABM point solutions locked under enterprise exclusivity contracts.

\---

## 2\. Geography \& Compliance Requirements (Criterion 4)

### Target Geographies (Band 10/8)

* North America (US, Canada)
* Western Europe (UK, Germany, France, Nordics)
* Major APAC Hubs (Singapore, Australia)

### Required Compliance \& Security Frameworks (Band 10)

* SOC 2 Type II Certification
* ISO 27001 Certification
* GDPR Compliance (for European operations)
* HIPAA Compliance (specifically for HealthTech SaaS accounts)

\---

## 3\. Client Product Lines \& Expansion Mapping (Criterion 10)

TargetOrate's enterprise product catalog for expansion assessment:

* **TargetOrate Core ABM Platform:** Account targeting and orchestration engine.
* **Intent Data \& Surge Intelligence Module:** Third-party buyer intent integration.
* **Pipeline Analytics \& Multi-Touch Attribution Suite:** Revenue attribution and closed-won analytics.

### Expansion Scoring Guide

* **Band 10 (3+ products across multiple departments):** Account has active use cases for Core ABM, Intent Data, and Pipeline Analytics across sales, marketing, and revenue ops.
* **Band 8 (2 products across units):** Applicable to Core ABM and Intent Data in marketing and sales.
* **Band 6 (1 product plausible):** Core ABM single-department use case.

\---

## 4\. Strategic Value Benchmarks (Criterion 11)

Evaluation tests for strategic account value:

* **Market Leader Test:** Company is in the top quartile by revenue ($$100\\text{M}+$) or employee count ($500+$) in its respective segment.
* **Referenceable Test:** Company publishes case studies, appears on public customer lists, or acts as a peer reference.
* **Partnership Potential Test:** Company appears on TargetOrate's strategic partner target ecosystem list (e.g., major cloud marketplace vendors, premier system integrators).

\---

## 5\. Win Probability \& Historical Benchmarks (Criterion 12)

* **Historical Win Rate Baseline:** TargetOrate's average win rate on similar mid-market/enterprise SaaS deals is $\\ge 50%$ when budget is confirmed.
* **Favorable Competitive Position:** Incumbent vendor is either absent, disliked, or tied to an expiring contract within 6 months.
* **Budget Status:** Formally confirmed via procurement notes, RFP issuance, or active executive sponsorship.
