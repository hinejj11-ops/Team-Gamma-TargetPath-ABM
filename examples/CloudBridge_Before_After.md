## What happens next for this account
- It has been in the capped band since 2026-03-25, when its renewal first fell within 24 months.
- It moves into the open window on its own once its renewal is under 12 months away, which is an As Of Date of 2027-03-26 or later. On 2027-03-25 it is still capped (exactly 12 months, inclusive). Rerun the gate with each new As Of Date.
- Evidence that the account is dissatisfied with its current vendor would move it up one band to the open window immediately. Evidence that it is satisfied would move it down to disqualified. Neither is in the record today.
