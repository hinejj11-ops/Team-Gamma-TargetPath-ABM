## Technology & Compliance Benchmarks — FIOR C3/C4

**Required technology categories**
- CRM / Sales Engagement: Salesforce CRM, HubSpot Enterprise, or Apollo.io.
- Cloud Infrastructure: AWS, Azure, or GCP.
- Data / Analytics: Snowflake, Databricks, or Segment.

**Preferred technologies**
- Intent / ABM: 6sense, Demandbase, or ZoomInfo Enterprise.
- Marketing Automation: Marketo or Pardot.

**Incompatible technology**
- Verified legacy proprietary on-prem databases without cloud connectors.
- Direct competing ABM point solutions under enterprise exclusivity.

**Compliance**
- SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001.
- GDPR when European operations apply.
- HIPAA additionally required for HealthTech SaaS.

If account technology/compliance evidence is too thin to verify these requirements, return **NS** under the FIOR rubric. Missing evidence is not a failed requirement.

## Intent & Trigger Definitions — FIOR C5/C6

**Intent**
- 10: 3+ distinct dated signal types in last 30 days.
- 8: 2 types in 30 days or 3+ in 90 days.
- 6: 1 type in 30 days or 2 in 90 days.
- 4: one generic type in 90 days.
- 2: verified no signals in 90 days.
- Blank/undated evidence: follow rubric NS/missing-data rules; do not manufacture recency.

Relevant signals include website/account activity, pricing or product research, case-study/ROI content, webinar/event engagement, third-party surge, and documented sales interactions.

**Triggers**
Relevant triggers include funding, platform migration, geographic/market expansion, regulatory/compliance mandate, senior GTM/technology leadership change, and M&A/integration. Preserve trigger dates; ≤6 months is materially stronger than 6–12 months or >12 months. Leadership changes require human review per rubric.

## Buying Committee & Relationship Benchmarks — FIOR C13/C14

Target contact coverage is ≥3 relevant stakeholders with at least one Director+ contact; preferred coverage includes a VP+ economic buyer and a functional champion. Role verification should be ≤6 months old.

A champion is an internal advocate with influence who is actively advancing the evaluation. A named contact alone is not a champion.

Typical influencers/end users include Demand Gen, Marketing Ops, RevOps, Sales Ops, enterprise sellers/BDRs, IT/Data, Security/Compliance, Finance, and Procurement.

## Competitive Context — FIOR C8/C12

- Best fit: greenfield or dissatisfaction with incumbent.
- Favorable: incumbent renewal ≤6 months.
- Neutral: build-vs-buy or active agency/tool evaluation.
- Risk: renewal >6 months or unknown.
- Hard disqualifier: verified exclusive/locked competitor with no actionable exit window.

Do not infer competitive favorability from vendor name alone.

## ABM Motion

- Tier 1: One:One — bespoke account research, executive POV, personalized business case and outreach.
- Tier 2: One:Few — 3–8 accounts sharing sub-industry, pain, trigger, and stakeholder pattern.
- Tier 3: One:Many — broader vertical/problem education with intent-based progression.

## Disqualifying / Gating Factors

- Verified annual budget / viable ACV below $50K with no approved expansion path.
- Wrong industry/use case. **Outsourced Digital Marketing is outside this synthetic example ICP** unless a consultant documents a strategic exception.
- Outside served regions or verified regulatory blocker.
- Verified incompatible technology as defined above.
- Both employee count and revenue materially outside the ICP with no strategic exception.
- Verified exclusive competitor lock-in with no actionable exit window.
- Documented material ethics, legal, brand-safety, or partnership-risk concern.
- Legally/regulatorily unable to contract or no legitimate B2B use case.

Ambiguous cases route to consultant review rather than automatic rejection.
